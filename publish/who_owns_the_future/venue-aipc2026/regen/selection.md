# selection.md — candidate selection for the b+ regeneration run (2026-10-04)

Three blind agents (general-purpose subagents, GLM; no access to the genesis
conversation or the owner's prompts; input = essay-with-holes.md only) each produced
11 replacement passages. Selection among AI outputs is permitted by the competition
rules. Selection criteria, fixed in advance:

1. Fidelity to the position stated in the seed.
2. Coherence with the essay's voice, motifs, and surrounding text.
3. Philosophical quality.
4. Independence: avoid candidates reproducing specific illustrative fingerprints of
   the genesis human prompts (a "three-year" priority gap; a 300-year extinction
   timescale; "pets"; a desert-island precedent). Note: candidate 1 independently
   wrote a "three years ahead" example for HOLE 7 — coincidence under blindness
   (marginal-speed examples are natural), but its HOLE 7 was passed over for a
   candidate with different figures, to keep the final text clean of accidental
   resemblance.

The owner's instruction — "pick the one the closest to the original idea" — was
applied at the level of the seeded POSITIONS (which are the permitted form of the
"original idea"); selection was not steered toward textual resemblance to the
human-supplied passages.
