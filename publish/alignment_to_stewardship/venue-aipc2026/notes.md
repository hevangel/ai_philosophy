## Verdict

**Ineligible as-is.** The operative FAQ test: "Did the AI originate and develop all the
substantive philosophical contributions that appear in the essay?" On the visible prompt
log, no — the human supplied the central critical argument (autonomy paradox) and, pending
the export, the constructive thesis concept (reverse-alignment stewardship), plus two
section-level seeds. An honest methodology report cannot clear the essay in its current
form. This is the same structural finding as `who_owns_the_future`, but sitting higher:
there the taints were embedded passages (examples, principles); here two of them are the
essay's spine.

**The chat export changes the boundary but not the method.** If the full conversation
shows the AI proposed the stewardship direction and/or articulated the paradox before the
owner's affirming turns, taints 2 (and possibly 1) shrink or dissolve; taints 3–4
(AGI-theocracy concept, Iran/Taliban claim) stand regardless. The export is required by
the rules (chat logs must be retained) and by the report either way.
