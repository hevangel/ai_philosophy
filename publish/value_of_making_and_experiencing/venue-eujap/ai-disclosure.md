# AI-use disclosure — "The Summit Is Not the Climb" (EUJAP submission)

EUJAP's norms for publishing with AI (https://eujap.uniri.hr/norms-for-publishing-with-ai/;
re-verified live 2026-10-13) state: "The Journal does not exclude the use of AI generated
text. However, all authors (including reviewers and editors) take full responsibility for its
factual accuracy and the proper acknowledgement of sources." The norms require identifying the
AI used and the extent of its contribution in the manuscript's acknowledgement section or on
the title page, using the negligible / modest / substantial scale.

**Contribution level: substantial** — per the norms, "the AI made several crucial suggestions
that shaped the research and the manuscript could not have been completed without it."
Narrative description, as the norms require for this level:

**Nature of AI involvement.** This paper was developed through dialogue between the human
author (Horace Chan) and large language models (GLM, Z.ai) across working sessions culminating
2026-10-14. The paper's thesis, distinctions, case selection, and arguments were directed,
selected, and revised by the author in that dialogue; the models drafted substantial portions
of the prose under his direction. Editorial passes (citation verification, completion of the
MacKenzie reference from the live journal record, conversion of two display equations to
plain-text notation, mechanical normalization) were performed by an AI agent (ZCode / GLM) on
2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14; a log is maintained in `../polish-notes.md`. No AI model is listed
as an author. The manuscript's own Acknowledgements section discloses this use in anonymized
form.

**Verification.** All 7 references were individually verified to exist via web search
(authors, titles, journals, volumes, pages, DOIs, publishers) before inclusion. No reference
is AI-invented.

**Responsibility.** The human author directed the work, reviewed the content, and takes full
moral and legal responsibility for it, including its factual accuracy and the proper
acknowledgement of sources.
