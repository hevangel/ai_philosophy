# Brief Introduction (簡介）

*The Art of Thinking* by Li Tianming (definitive edition)

Author: Li Tianming
Editors: Rong Ziyou （戎子由）, Liang Peilin （梁沛霖）

A completely revised edition — the definitive edition.

** *The Art of Thinking* has gone through 26 printings since publication (the Beijing and Taiwan editions not counted). From the 27th printing onward, the book appears in this completely revised definitive edition.

** The body of the book consists of five essays by Li; in the new edition every page has been reviewed and revised.

** The revisions include:

(1) New layout — the structure of the essays has been adjusted to bring them closer to perfection.

(2) Added content — the notes have gained a great many pointers to ideas; the main text has gained new sections and passages, including "The Logic of Thinking vs the Science of Thinking," "On Critical Thinking and Creative Thinking," and "Jesus, Buddha, the Dao, and the Heart-mind," plus a new "Postscript to the Definitive Edition."

(3) Perfected wording — Mr Li's prose is famously sharp and his word choice precise; the new edition has reviewed the wording throughout, striving for ever greater refinement.

** Beyond the Main Part, editorial revisions include:

(4) Clearing away the grit — the speeches in the "Li–Horner debate" have been checked and transcription errors corrected, and so on.

(5) Improved format — the whole book is now set horizontally, for convenient reading of mathematical-logic formulas, and key points are displayed in an appropriate typeface to help the reader grasp the sense.

(6) New appendix — Dr Lü Xiang's "The Tyranny of Language and the Art of Thinking," exploring the philosophical ambition behind Li's art of thinking, and Professor Chen Qiangli's "On Li Tianming's Method of Thinking," arguing that Li "opened up a new field in the methodology of thinking," are both now included as appendices in the definitive edition.

— Editorial Department, Ming Pao Publications

To lack knowledge is merely to be ignorant; to be clumsy at thinking is to be stupid.

"Objects" are impermanent and can be destroyed, but "events" are permanent and cannot be destroyed. If you once loved each other, and once shared the happiest of times together, then not even God can "erase" those facts.

The essence of love is to meet again the other part of oneself. Many people hurry anxiously through the world, searching here and there, precisely in order to meet again the other part of themselves. Love is the most fundamental mutual dependence for survival — depending on each other for survival in the vast universe.

===

The only "formula" of the art of thinking is this: the art of thinking has no formula.
