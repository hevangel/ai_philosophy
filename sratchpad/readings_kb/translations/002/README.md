# Translations — book 002: Li Tianming, 《李天命的思考藝術》 (*The Art of Thinking*)

*Owner ruling (PLAN.md, decision 5): book 002's content enters the knowledge base in English. This folder holds faithful, complete English translations of the parts of book 002 actually drawn on for the Logic & Critical Thinking slice, made from the extracted Chinese text in `data/philosophy_readings/markdown/002 - 李天命的思考藝術/`. Entries in `entries/logic-and-critical-thinking/` cite these translations, not the Chinese files directly. Translation conventions: Li's coined terms are kept in Chinese in brackets at first use （語理分析 semantic analysis, 語害 linguistic harm, 對確 valid); names use the book's own romanisations where given; OCR-corrupted characters in the extraction are marked [thus] where reconstructed, and formulas lost to OCR are flagged rather than invented.*

## Data-quality warning carried into every citation

The 002 extraction folder is **interleaved with another book**: alongside the 21 Chinese files of *The Art of Thinking* sit 38 files of an English pop-culture volume (*The Simpsons and Philosophy* — ch01 bookcover/section stubs, ch02–ch38 Simpsons chapters), evidently a merged extraction. The Chinese files are internally coherent and complete for this book; the Simpsons files are not part of book 002 and are ignored here — but any future automated pass over the folder by filename order alone will mix the two books. See the book map (`book-maps/002-the-art-of-thinking.md`) for the full per-file account.
