# The Philosophy Readings Knowledge Base

Most philosophy textbooks march forward through their own chapters. This knowledge base is organized the other way: around **standing problems** — the debates that keep reopening — each built from what the sources actually say. Every problem entry opens with a 90-second lecture version (the front door), then sets out the positions with faithful premise-by-premise reconstructions of the canonical arguments, the objections at the strength their authors state them, the thought experiments, a source ladder from first lecture to primary text, and an honest account of where the debate stands. Unsettled debates stay unsettled.

The whole base lives in the interactive explorer — open **Philosophy Readings** on the site (the `/readings` route) and browse by problem, subfield, book, or lecture. The markdown under `entries/` and `book-maps/` remains the source of truth; `data.json` is rebuilt from it by `scratchpad/readings_kb/build_data.py`.

## Coverage

- **53 problem entries** across 9 subfields (philosophy of law is deferred — folder 039 holds the wrong book)
- **26 book maps**, chapter by chapter, with every extraction caveat kept visible
- **Lectures:** 129 of 817 Carneades transcripts assigned to entries (77 front doors); 686 unassigned — candidates for future entries, not discards
- **Data quality:** 8 of the 26 catalogue folders contain a different book than their name claims — read [DATA_QUALITY.md](DATA_QUALITY.md) before trusting the catalogue
