# Book map — 012 (second folder): Robert E. Goodin and Philip Pettit (eds.), *Contemporary Political Philosophy: An Anthology*, 2nd edition (Blackwell, 2005/2006) — RECONSTRUCTION, mostly stubs

*Book layer · Political philosophy. Companion to `012-contemporary-political-philosophy-anthology-3rd-ed.md`, which maps the 3rd edition (2019) of the same anthology held in the sibling folder. The two 012s are kept distinct everywhere in this slice: this folder is **not** a second copy of the 3rd edition and must not be cited as if its readings were read.*

**Extraction note — this "book" is largely not here.** The folder
(`data/philosophy_readings/markdown/012 - Contemporary Political Philosophy - An Anthology/`)
contains no epub extraction: per its `_meta.json`, the book had "no epub on [the]
libgen mirror; pdf retry pending," and on the owner's instruction (2026-10-04) it was
*reconstructed* — `toc.md` is a sourced table of contents for the 1st (1997) and 2nd
(2005/2006) editions compiled from the Library of Congress TOC, Internet Archive scan
metadata, and Google Books (**reconstruction, not book text**), and files ch01–ch48
correspond one-to-one to the 2nd edition's 48 readings. Of those 48 files, **46 are
citation stubs** (~270–520 bytes each: title, author, original citation, and a best
access point — the reading itself is absent because it is under copyright and no open
copy was located). Only **two readings are present in full**, both fetched from openly
hosted copies on 2026-10-04:

- **ch21 — Judith Jarvis Thomson, "A Defense of Abortion"** (47k chars; full text, from a University of Colorado course page). Also present in full in the 3rd edition (file ch35 there).
- **ch24 — Isaiah Berlin, "Two Concepts of Liberty"** (121k chars; full text, from a Utah Tech course PDF). Also present in full in the 3rd edition (file ch38 there).

Everything below is therefore a map of the 2nd edition's *contents* (from `toc.md`),
with each reading's status in this folder tagged. **Stub = not readable in this
corpus folder; do not write from it.** Where the same reading exists in full in the
3rd-edition folder, that is noted — the 3rd-edition text is the citable one.

## Coverage
Auditable coverage of this folder is: the two full texts (Thomson, Berlin), the
reconstructed TOC (`toc.md`, itself fully sourced and explicit about its provenance),
and the stub metadata. The 1st-edition TOC in `toc.md` is marked there as partly
inferred and should be treated as less certain than the LOC-sourced 2nd-edition list
above. For every substantive purpose in this KB slice, the 3rd-edition folder is the
working copy of the anthology; this reconstruction's value is bibliographic (edition
history, dropped/added readings) and as the corpus's only record of the 2nd edition's
contents. If the 2nd edition's PDF ever lands, `toc.md` states it must be deleted and
this map rewritten from the text.
