Robert E. Goodin and Philip Pettit (eds.), *Contemporary Political
Philosophy: An Anthology*, Blackwell. 1st ed. 1997 (x + 648 pp.,
ISBN 1-55786-842-5); 2nd ed. 2005/2006 (xiv + 754 pp., ISBN 1-4051-3065-2).
Note: Thomas Pogge did NOT co-edit this anthology — he co-edited the
companion *A Companion to Contemporary Political Philosophy*; his essay
"Migration and Poverty" appears in the 2nd ed. as a contributor.

The owner read it around 2006–2011, so the 2nd edition TOC below is the
likely one; the 1st ed. list follows for completeness.

## 2nd-edition changes (from diffing the two lists)

- Adds the whole Part VIII, International Affairs; Held's democracy essay and
  Barry's global-justice essay move there from earlier parts.
- Dropped from the 1st ed.: Offe & Ronge, Rawls "The Domain of the Political",
  Marshall "Citizenship and Social Class", Roemer, Elshtain.
- Notable additions: Scott, Barry "Chance, Choice and Justice", Waldron
  "Superseding Historic Injustice", Beitz, Skinner "A Third Concept of
  Liberty", G. A. Cohen "Are Freedom and Equality Compatible?", Galston,
  Parfit "Equality or Priority?", Arneson, Okin, Anscombe, Margalit & Raz,
  Rawls "The Law of Peoples", Luban, Keohane, Pogge.
- Publisher blurb says "thirteen additional texts"; the literal diff shows
  ~15 added. Uncertain.

## What the book covers (sourced)

- 2nd ed. publisher blurb (via Google Books): "This authoritative collection
  of the seminal texts in post-war political philosophy has now been updated
  and expanded. Reprints key articles, mainly unabridged, touching upon the
  nature of the state, democracy, justice, rights, liberty, equality and
  oppression."
- 1st ed. back cover (via Amazon): thirty-nine mostly unabridged essays on
  the state, democracy, justice, rights, liberty, equality, and oppression;
  mixes political, legal, and economic writing with philosophy; includes
  continental as well as analytic thinkers, plus feminists, Marxists and
  other radicals alongside traditional liberals; designed to pair with the
  editors' *Companion to Contemporary Political Philosophy*.
- A 3rd edition (Wiley, 2019, 49 selections) also exists — not the owner's.
